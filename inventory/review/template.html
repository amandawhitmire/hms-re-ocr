HMS Name Review

Each card shows where a taxonomic name sits on the page image, what Google Vision read there, and the closest verified name. When a paper has the same reading on several pages, they share one card with every clip shown; one choice covers them all, and any clip that differs can be decided separately. Decide what the page actually shows. Choices save automatically and are shared with anyone who can edit this page.

  • OCR error: the page shows something other than what Vision read.
  • On the page: Vision read it correctly; the author wrote it this way.
  • Not a name: the flagged words are not a taxonomic name.
  • Can't tell: too faint, cut off or illegible to decide.
  • Use closest name for search: a separate choice. It records the name people should find the paper by, whatever the page says.
