Sample preparation and analysis: All samples were diluted with
one to three drops of distilled water, homogenized by ultra sound, and
then spun down for three to four minutes in a high speed

microcentrifuge. The homogenates were absorbed into whatmans's *1
filter paper, and analyzed using horizontal starch gel electrophoresis.
Gel preparation consisted of pouring a mixture of 35 g starch (Sigma)
suspended in 80 ml of cold buffer solution into 220 ml of boiling
buffer solution. The final 300ml volume was mixed vigorously,
degassed, and poured into 17 cm by 17 cm by 1 cm molds. The gels
were cooled to 4 degrees C before loading the samples. Enzymatic
activity was detected at the following loci: phosphoglucoisomerase.
(Pgi-1 and Pgi-2), phosophoglucomutase (Pgm); tetrazolium oxidase
(To), hexokinase (Hk-1 and HK-2), malate dehydrogenase (Mdh-1 and
Mdh-2), malic enzyme (Me), acid phosphotase (Acp), and xanthine
dehydrogenase (Xdh). Only Pgi-1, Pgm, To, Hk-2, and Mdh-1 were
scored. The other loci were either not resolved well enough to be
scorable, or, in the case of Hk-1 and Pgi-2, were detectable only in
very small individuals. The buffer systems used are described in
Ayala et al (1973). Buffer "C" (Tris, citrate, EDTA, pH 7.0) was used to
resolve all scored loci. The enzyme stains used were all from
