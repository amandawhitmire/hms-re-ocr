Table I gives the observed and expected heterozygosities, the
inbreeding coefficients, Fst, and Nm for each locus. None of the
observed heterozygosities are significantly different from what is
expected under Hardy-Weinburg conditions, except for Hk at Moss.
Landing, where there is a significant excess of heterozygotes. The
values for Nm vary considerably between loci, with Pgi, Pgm, and HK
all giving values between 15 and 25 migrants per generation, but To
giving a value of over 200 migrants per generation. The value for To
is, however, derived from allele frequencies from Morro Bay and Port
Hueneme only, while the other estimates are derived from frequencies
from at least three locations. The average values for Nm are 73.2
using all four loci, and 20.2 if To is left out.
