Discussion

Geographic variation:

The allele frequencies at each location sampled are clearly very
similar, and the estimated number of migrants per generation between
sample sites is quite high, as might be expected for an intertidal
species with a pelagic larval period. However, the individuals sampled

probably do not belong to a single panmictic population, and the
results suggest that the dispersal patterns for barnacle larva are
quite complex. The Pgi allele frequencies are significantly different.
