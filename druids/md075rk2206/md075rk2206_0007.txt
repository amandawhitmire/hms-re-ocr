Almost all Crustacea have one or more pairs of glandular
appendages on the midgut that are thought to take part in
digestion. In Malacostraca digestive juices are produced
almost entirely by these tissues. However, in other Crustacea
production presumably takes place in both the glandular
appendages and the midgut, although experimental evidence
is lacking on this point. In Balanus nubilis it was shown
that digestive carbohydrases are located throughout the fore-
gut and midgut with concentrations in the foregut, glandulae
pancreaticae, and first part of the midgut.
