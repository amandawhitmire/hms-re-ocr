Figure 3. Quantitative measures of changes in orientation of
limpets high (14 to 18 feet) and low (9 to 12 feet)

data in Table 2. Duration of the period of splash

black bars on the splash diagram and the numbers
just above them. The area under the line on the
graph of splash hitting the rock shows which groups
(high or low) are being splashed. When the line
roaches its maximum, both groups are being wetted.
