High Tide Observations:

Behavior

At high tide, the fish within one meter from the edge of the pools were all
sitting very stationary on horizontal rock faces or sometimes positioned obviously in
shallow cracks in the granite. I saw minimal movement at this time, and I was even
able to snorkel directly over the fish without apparent disturbance. I did not see fish
hidden in the algae or beneath rocks or debris, but saw them instead sitting in
exposed areas of the study site. On 10 of the 11 high tide observations made, I
discovered one or more large sculpin > 10 cm in length in the bottoms of the pools,
and once I observed an eel blenny in Pool 3. Surfperch were also seen swimming in
the area over my study site.
