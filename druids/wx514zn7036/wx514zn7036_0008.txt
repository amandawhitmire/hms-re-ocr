Mid Tide Observations:

Behavior

On two occasions I watched Area A next to tidepool 3 as the tide level moved
lower than this point. Of the 7 fish that were subsequently observed to be in the pool
when it was completely isolated, I saw 4 individuals of Pool 3 in this area
approximately 20 cm away from the lip of the pool. The surge was high as the waves
moved into this area, and at times the fish were no more than 2 cm under the water.
At one time the surge moved out so quickly as to leave one individual stranded
without water for several seconds until the next wave came in. At this time the fish
moved towards the lip and eventually down into the pool. Activity seemed to be
directed to moving slowly down into the pool with each lower surge, and occasionally
I saw some of the fish exhibit a feeding behavior on some neighboring algae. On
one instance I saw a tagged fish from Pool 3 inhabiting Pool 4. Before the pools
were isolated, however, the fish moved from Pool 4 into its original pool and
remained there till the next high tide.

rock and closer to the surface. As was observed during the transition as the tide went
