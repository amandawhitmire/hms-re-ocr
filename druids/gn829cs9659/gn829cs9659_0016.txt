Table 1. Solutions employed.

pH's of all solutions employed ranged between 7.0-7.3, osmolarity
between 400-420 mosm.

30 mM glucose was added to each solution prior to experiments.

Dantrolene sodium was obtained from W. Gilly, D-600 from T. Scheuer.
All other chemicals were obtained from Sigma Chemical.

All experiments were conducted in fish ringer except those necessitating

O Na. and K-Glutamate. When gilai ringers were used, fish ringer was
used initially as a control. Fish and gilat ringers did not give
differing control twitches and tetani.
