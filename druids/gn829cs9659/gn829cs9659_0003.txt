MATERIALS & METHODS

Preparation

Specimens of Citharichthys sordidus were obtained fishing off
Lover's Point, Monterey Bay. Fish were maintained prior to use in
large outdoor tanks with circulating, fresh sea water.

Physiology

Measurement of isometric tension:

Experiments were performed on isolated single muscle units (one
muscle, one bone, and one spine) mounted near their in vivo resting
length in a 10 ml plexiglass chamber. The ends of the muscle close
to the myotomes was pinned to the sylgard (Dow Corning) floor and the
cut spine was tied to a force transducer with silk thread (Gilly &
Hut, 1980). Contraction was elicited by application of longitudinally
oriented shocks generated by a Grass S-6 stimulator via platinum/

platinum black electrodes.

Pulse duration varied between 0.5-2.0 ms

and field strength from 3-35 volts/cm. Electrodes were mounted on a

micromanipulator and could be positioned close to the muscle ends.
