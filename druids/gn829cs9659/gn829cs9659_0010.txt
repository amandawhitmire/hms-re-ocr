Membrane potential is held at a certain holding potential

and 20 ms depolarizations are delivered with increasing amplitude
until contractile responses are seen through the microscope.

In this

fashion, contractile threshold can be determined.

Results of this

procedure at several holding potentials in both Ca-containing and Ca-
free media are plotted in figure 9. Contraction occurs in O-Ca
medium at a fairly normal threshold voltage until holding potential
is reduced to quite depolarized values. At -20 mV holding potential,
contraction fails in the Ca-free medium. Failure is probably due to
inactivation of E-C coupling in a voltage-dependent manner, and not
related directly to calcium.

The absence of contraction noted in figure 8 in 0 Ca/5 mM EGTA

medium is probably due to depolarization of the membrane due to low

calcium concentration.

Resting potentials measured in the Ca-free

medium tended to be substantially less negative than the normal

abolish contraction is consistent with the view that action potentials
