What are the functional roles of these fibers in mediating
behavior of the fish? The fast fibers are likely to be used in any
fast fin movement, such as the characteristic burying, extension/

retraction of fins, and the initial elevation of fins in the rheotaxtic

response. In order to understand the functioning of the fast fibers

in relation to the other muscles that control fin movement,

knowledge of the basic properties of the other fin muscles is

necessary. Speculation as to how the sand dab uses its muscles for

different movements then would be more profitable.

Sand dab fast twitch muscles represent a high ly unique solution

to the problem of fast, yet finely controlled movement with a
relatively simple motor neuron system. The mechanics of this system
compared to the mechanics of the typical vertebrate, action potential
propogating, singly or dually innervated system will be of special
comparative interest. Such an analysis will lead to a more

comprehensive understanding of muscle diversity.
