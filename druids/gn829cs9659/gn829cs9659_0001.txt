ABSTRACT

In a sand dab fin musc le preparation, the electrical and mechanical
properties of a fast twitch muscle have been studied. The "long"
(presumably multiply innervated) fast fibers have resting potentials
of -75 to -80 mV and are taken to threshold for contraction (-40 mV)
by depolarizing junction potentials.

All-or-nothing action potentials

are not seen, even when suprathreshold shocks are applied. Twitches
are very fast; contraction reaches a peak within 10 ms and relaxation
is complete within 20 ms at 18°c. Direct stimulation produces unfused
tetant up to at least 200 Hz.
