dish containing sea water and 1-2% EtOH solution. The squid were

of the mantle. The internal organs were removed, and the skin was pinned

was immersed in cold artificial seawater (460mM NaC1, 10mM CaCl2, 10mM
KC1, 50mM MgC12,10mM HEPES, pH 7.8) to prevent the skin from desiccating.
The first two dermal layers including the chromatophore layer were
removed with forceps and the iridophore layer was used.

Morphological studies. All the iridophore tissue observed was treated
for at least 15 min with 1% Triton X-100 in KGE (KGE: .3M potassium
gluconate, .33M glycine, 2mM magnesium chloride, 2mM EGTA, pH 6.8).
Unused cell tissue was preserved at sub-zero temperature KGE with 50%
glycerol and 1% Triton. Both preserved and fresh tissue were examined
under transmitted light and incident light. Pictures were taken using a
35mm Olympus or a Nikon camera mounted directly to the microscope.
Iridophore cells were also video taped as they were illuminated from
different angles (optical fibers were used). Video taped data was analyzed.
and printed using a MegaVision image processor.

Polarized light analysis. Iridophore cells and platelets were observed
under a polarized light microscope. The 40X lens was used.

Differential Interference Contrast. Iridophore cells were examined
with DIC under a 40X lens. DIC was set on max extinction and then on either
side of max extinction.
