SDS-PAGE Gels

Gels were set up using the Bio-Rad Mini Protean II Electrophoresis gel system.
Samples were dissolved in 2X Laemmli sample buffer and heated to 95°C for 6 minutes.
Gels were run with 16mA/gel for approximately 1.5 hours or until the dye front reached
the end of the gel. 10% and 5% SDS-PAGE gels were only run for samples from the
sucrose gradient.

Tricine SDS-PAGE Gels

As described by Judd in the Protein Protocol's Handbook, the separating gel was
composed of 1.34 ml water, 2 ml separating gel buffer, 2 ml of 1X crosslinker
separating/spacer gel acrylamide, 640 μL glycerol, 2 μL TEMED, and 100 μL 10%
ammonium persulfate. The recipe for the spacer gel was 1.38 ml water, 1 ml
separating/spacer gel buffer, 0.6 ml 1X crosslinker separating/spacer gel acrylamide, 1
μL TEMED, and 10 μL 10% ammonium persulfate. The stacking gel recipe was
composed of 2.06 ml water, 0.38 ml 1.0M Tris buffer, 0.5 ml stacking gel acrylamide, 12
μL 0.5M EDTA, 1.5 μL TEMED, and 30 μL 10% ammonium persulfate. Samples were
dissolved in 2X sample buffer and heated to 95°C for 6 minutes. 10X cathode running
buffer was made of 1M Trizma base, 1M tricine, and 1% SDS and had a pH of 8.25. The
10X stock of anode buffer was composed of 2M Trizma base with a pH of 8.9. Tricine
gels were run with 16mA/gel for approximately 3 hours when the dye front reached the

end of the gel.
