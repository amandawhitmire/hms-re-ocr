When the preamplification cycles were complete, I ran 8 μL of the preamplification
solution on a 1% agarose gel to check the efficacy of the preamplification step. The
remaining 12 μL of the preamplification solution were diluted 1:20 with 0.1X TE. 3 μL
of this diluted preamplification solution were mixed with 1 μL 10X PCR buffer, 1 μL 2
mM dNTP's, 0.25 μL 10 μM labeled EcoRI primer (random bases AGG or ACT), 0.25
μL 30 μM Msel primer (random bases TGA or TAC), 0.05 μL Taq, and 4.45 μL dd H₂O
to give a total reaction volume of 10 μL. We prepared the reactions on ice, spun them for
a few seconds in a microfuge, transferred the samples back to ice, and then transferred the
samples directly into a PCR machine preheated to 95° C. The 36 PCR cycles were as

indicated in Table 1.

The labeled EcoRI primers were prepared beforehand. For a 25 μL sample, 11.25
μL dd H₂O, 2.5 μL 100 μM primer stock, 2.5 μL polynucleotide kinase buffer, 7.5 μL y
33P ATP, and 1.25 μL T4 polynucleotide kinase (9.30 U/μL) were added together. The
solution was incubated at 37° C for 30 minutes and then at 65° C for 15 minutes. We then

stored the labeled primers in a lead pig in a freezer designated for radioactivity.
