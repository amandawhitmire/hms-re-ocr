1 VOLUME T. CRASSICORNIS

(500 ml.)

+

1 VOLUME 1M SODIUM CITRATE

IN DISTILLED WATER

OR

1 VOLUME IM SUCROSE IN 0.45 N
MILLIPORE FILTERED SEA WATER

MACERATE IN WARING BLENDER (APPROXIMATELY 15 MINUTES)

FILTER THROUGH CHEESECLOTH WITH VACUUM

FILTRATE (NEMATOCYSTS, TISSUE,
CITRATE OR SUCROSE)

CENTRIFUGE (15 MINUTES, 5°C, 750G)

WASH, RESUSPEND, AND RECENTRIFUGE

IN IM SODIUM CITRATE OR IM SUCROSE
UNTIL SUPERNATANT CLEAR

DISCARD RESIDUE

DISCARD SUPERNATANT

DRAIN THOROUGHLY; RINSE RESIDUAL
ISOLATION MEDIUM FROM INSIDE SURFACE

OF CENTRIFUGE TUBE WITHOUT DISTURBING PELLET

DISCHARGE NEMATOCYSTS BY ADDING A VOLUME OF DISTILLED WATER
APPROXIMATELY EQUIVALENT TO PELLET VOLUME; LET STAND 12-18hrs.

CENTRIFUGE ( 2000 G, 15 MINUTES)

SUPERNATANT IS TOXIN IN DISTILLED WATER

DISCARD PELLET
