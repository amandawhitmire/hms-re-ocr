PCR

Blue marlin specific primers, RyR Bam (5'-CTGGATCCTCAATCTCATC-3'
(REVERSE)) and RyR Eco (5'-GAGAATTCCTGAACTCCTGG-3' (FORWARD)), were
used at 1 mM concentrations each. 2.5 mM MgCl2, 200-500 ng of cDNA
template, and .125 UM dNTPs were used. A hot start procedure was done for the
reactions in which .5 u (units) Taq DNA polymerase was added once the
reactions had reached 80°C in a Perkin Elmer 480 thermal cycler. PCR
conditions were 35 cycles at 95°C for 1 min, 20 sec; 50°C for 1 min, 30 sec; 72°C
for 1 min; 72°C for 1 min, 30 sec; 72°C for 7 min delay.

PCR products were precipitated as described for cDNA and were run on a
1% TAE low melting agarose gel. Products at the predicted size (approx. 450 bp)
were cut from the gel and stored in labeled microcentrifuge tubes at -20°C.

Madison, WI) according to the manufacturer's specifications with some indicated
changes. 200 vl of high efficiency XLI- Blue Escherichia coli competent cells
