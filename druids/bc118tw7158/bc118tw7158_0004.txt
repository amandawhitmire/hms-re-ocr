The principal object of this study was to inves-
tigate the mechanism involved in homing behavior. In
addition two other aspects of homing which have received
little investigation are included in this study. These
are the homing behavior of animals from high and low
elevations in the intertidal, and the behavior of
different size classes of limpets.

were made at Pescadero Point on the Monterey Peninsula,
California during April and May, 1966.
