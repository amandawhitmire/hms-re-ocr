FLOURIMETRIC ASSAY

Urechis caupo and Ascidia ceratodes eggs show a transport activity
characteristic of MDR activity. As seen by figures 4 and 5, eggs incubated
only with Rhodamine B exhibit less fluorescence then those incubated in dye
pius verapamil, the MDR activity inhibitor. Urechiseggs normally contain
10%-25% of the Rhodamine B concentrated by eggs in which the transport
activity is inhibited. Ascidianeggs normally concentrate only 25% - 50% of
that in with which transport activity is inhibited.

Experiments conducted on Strongylocentrotus purpuratus, Lytechinus
pictusand Dendraster excentricus showed no detectable MDR transport
activity. As shown by figures 6 and 7, there is little difference between
samples incubated with and without Verapamil. Therefore these organisms
do not appear to have the MDR activity.

IMMUNOBLOTS

Western blots on Urechis caupoeggs, Ascidianeggs, and Mytilus
californianisgill and mantle tissue showed a positive cross reaction with
the C219 mammalian monoclonal antibody. The bands were 142 KD, 146 KD,
and 138 KD respectively, indicating that these organisms have a protein that
is different, though close in molecular weight to the 170 kD mammalian p-
givcoprotein responsible for MDR activity. In addition the Mytilus gill and
mantel tissue and the Ascidianeggs had cross reacting bands for proteins of
different molecular weights. The mussel had a 200 kD band and both had 34
KD bands.
