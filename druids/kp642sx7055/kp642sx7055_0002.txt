in the past numerous studies on the effects of various pollutants on
marine organisms have been conducted. However, few detoxification
mechanisms have been identified or are understood. Among these are
cytochrome P-450 detoxification; glutathione conjugation mechanisms;
stress proteins, such as heat shock proteins, and mixed function oxidases
(MFOS). Some of these mechanisms are not active in lower marine
invertebrates. In fact, little work can explain which or how many
mechanisms are employed in many invertebrates.
