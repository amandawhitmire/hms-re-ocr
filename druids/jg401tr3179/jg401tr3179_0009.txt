Discussion

Characteristic radular grazing marks were seen a number of times on
slides from Tegula -accessible patio stones in the field. This implies that
Tegula does not only eat gametophytes under controlled laboratory
conditions, but will eat them off glass slides in the field as well. Significant.
differences between treatments at the 7.6 m site show that this grazing
behavior could cause some of the observed gametophyte mortality. Tegula,
however, was far from the dominant factor dictating gametophyte mortality
or survival in my experiments. Even in the plots where Tegula was kept
out, almost 95% mortality was observed after a week. It is estimated that a
