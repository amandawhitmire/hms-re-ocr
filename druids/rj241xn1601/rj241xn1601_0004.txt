METHODS

Copepods were obtained from Monterey Bay, Cali-
fornia weekly in late April and throughout May, 1969.
With a meter net of 0.333 mm mesh aperture both ver-
tical and oblique tows were made from depths of 25 m.
to the surface. Immediately after capture indivi-
duals were placed in pre-cooled sea water until sor-
ting; adult females, used in most experiments, were.
kept in the laboratory in membrane filtered (0.8u)
sea water. During the last two weeks of May only
stage five copepodite females were available and these
were used with no apparent change in results.
perimental animals were maintained in semi-darkness
in large, uncovered glass jars at an approximate
density of 1 animal/20 ml. The sea water, kept at
13° C. was changed twice weekly. The copepods were
fed with mixed phytoplankton cultures of Skeletonema
costatum, Thalassiosira sp., and Chaetoceros sp.
They were removed from feeding jars at least three
hours prior to the beginning of any experiment.
Under these conditions the animals survived up to

six weeks.
