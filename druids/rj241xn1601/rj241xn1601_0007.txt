DISCUSSION

Although concentrations below 50 ppb do not
clearly affect the copepods in 24 hours, the 48 hour
experiments show a significant effect at as low as
10 ppb DDT. This could indicate a cumulative effect
with time until a critical level is reached, or that
with low concentrations, a longer time period is re-
quired to cause the metabolic damage which presumably
kills or incapacitates the animals. Copepods were in-
cubated up to 144 hours at 1 ppb before succumbing
to the pesticide; at this point the controls were
still lively. As the concentrations were increased,
the time required before visible effect was observed,

decreased .

Concentrations as high as 10,000 ppb were tested.
but even at this level all animals were not killed in
24 hours, although 100% were affected.

Because so

much of the DDT precipitated at levels above 1000 ppb,
most experiments were conducted at lower concentra-
tions to ensure as much accuracy and retention of the
original concentration as possible.

This method was

not infallible, however, as white particles presumed
to be DDT or associated with DDT were observed on the
appendages and antennae of the copepods incubated

for 48 hours at concentrations as low as 30 ppb.
