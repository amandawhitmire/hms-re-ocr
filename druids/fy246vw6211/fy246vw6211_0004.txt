Where high magnification (400X) is available, the
number of prominent spines on the exopodite of the second
antenna provides a definite morphological characteristic
helpful in partially separating the five species. The
eight antennal spines on P. ochotensis are evenly separated
and readily distinguishable; however the most distal spines
of the other species are close together and usually are
not discernable except under higher magnification.
