DISCUSSION

The results show that in the study area. Anthopleura
elegantissima is distributed in a definite gradient from
the outfall. In the immediate area of the outfall no
anemones can be found while as one moves farther away, the
populations increase in size. This suggests that there
is a relationship between sewage effluent and the distri-
bution of anemones. This suggestion is consistent with
the results of the field studies where rocks with resettled
anemones were placed out in the field: the anemones detached
at a rate dependent on distance from the outfall. Also,
a chemical analysis of the area demonstrates the same trend
in the distribution of chlorine, phosphorus, and salinity.
Thus it appears that sewage is responsible for the distri-
