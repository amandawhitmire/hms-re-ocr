METHODS AND MATERIALS

Current studies were made in early April, 1970 by utilizing
dye packets containing twenty grams of fluorescein dye. These
were thrown into the water from the rocky precipice directly
behind the outfall and at distances of 5, 15, 25, and 35 feet
from the end of the pipe. Because it is difficult to follow
dye packets in heavy surf, drift bottles were also employed,
using the same pattern of distribution as described above.
These were light-weight color-coded plastic bottles which
had been half-filled with fresh water so that they would
remain buoyant. The packets and bottles were followed from
0630 to 1830 on a single day, April 13. Low tide on that
day was at 1130.

During the same period studies were made of the distri-
butional pattern of salinity, chlorine, and phosphorus in
the study area. Chlorine and phosphorus were used as indica-
tors of the sewage field because they are known to be present
in high concentrations in Pacific Grove primary effluent.

Methods (Strickland and Parsons, 1965). Salinity measurements
