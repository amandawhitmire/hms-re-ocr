To determine the LT50, anemones in groups of ten
were placed in gallon jars with various concentrations
of Pacific Grove (chlorinated) and Monterey (unchlorinated)
sewage. The following concentrations were used: 100%.
50%, 33%, 20%, and 10%. The 100% sewage was corrected
for osmotic difference by adding 35 grams of Wards Instant
Ocean, per liter. A sea water control and a 50% freshwater
control for possible osmotic effects were also utilized.
For each of the jugs, the water was changed every day and
aerated, and the bottles were submerged in running
sea water so that the temperature would remain at a constant
13-14 degrees Centrigrade.
