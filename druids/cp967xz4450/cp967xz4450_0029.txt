Finally, the finite element analysis graphs also

demonstrate that equal length Climatula and A. mitra shells
develop very different stress concentrations. Comparison of
figure 20 A and B (and C and D) show that at each grasping
orientation the stresses in Climatule are much greater than
those for A. mitra. Again, the most likely explanation involves
the thinner scar layer in Callisella limatule at equal shell
lengths. With less shell material to contain the stress, stress

levels clearly rise in Climatula shells and cause fracture

much more quickly.
