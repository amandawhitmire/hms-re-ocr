B. FATIGUE-LOADING EXPERIMENTS

Colliselle limatule and Calliselle pelta shells were

loaded repeatedly for five seconds at a time for up to a
maximum of 200 cycles. The loads applied were some fraction
of the predicted breaking force ranging from around 10-90%.

the results of part A for the two limpet species in question.
