C. COMPARISONS OF SHELL DIMENSIONS WITHIN AND AMONG

SPECIES

Measurements at the length and width of the scar and

margin revealed (figure 12) that for each species the shell

width at the scar was significantly smaller than the distance
which must be spanned by a cheliped in the other three
orientations (p<.001 in all three cases). Also, the comparison
of sizes among species (figure 13), using shells with similar
lengths (margin length= 18-21mm) revealed that Climatule
has the smallest average width at the scar(p<.001), with A.
mitra substantially wider at the scar than the others.
