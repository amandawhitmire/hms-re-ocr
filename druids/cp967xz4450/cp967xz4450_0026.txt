A possible explanation to the mechanism by which shells.
loaded at the margin fractured first at the muscle scar lies in
the muscle scar microstructure. Anaylses of the fractured

crossed-lamellar structure at the muscle scar reveal that the

lamellae are oriented with the surface of each sheet lying
normal to the shell's outer surface. In this manner a crack may
travel along the scar (between the primary lamellae), leaving
behind a smooth fracture surface. This situation is analogous
to splitting plywood along the grain (Currey, 1974,1980), in the
