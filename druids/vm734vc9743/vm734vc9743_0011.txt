Statistics

Accumulation and toxicity data were analyzed using ANOVA to detect significant difference. A
Student-Newman-Keuls two-tailed test (α = 0.05) was used to determine which treatments were
significantly different. Efflux data was analyzed for equality of slopes using SYSTAT.
