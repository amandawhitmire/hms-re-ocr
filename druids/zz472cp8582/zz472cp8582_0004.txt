Secretion Production Experiments:

A total of eighty C. canaliculatum (different from the thirty
used in the above experiments) were collected on May 4, 1988 and
induced to release the yellow substance that day. To collect the
exudate, a snail was held with the aperature up while a Pycnopodia
tube foot was applied to the head of the animal. The snail then
retracted into its shell while secreting the product mixed in
seawater. This mixture was collected using a micropipet and

stored in a refrigerated flask.
