MATERIALS AND METHODS

Experimental Animals:

Calliostoma canaliculatum and Pycnopodia helianthoides (here-
after Pycnopodia) were collected in the kelp beds off of Hopkins
Marine Life Refuge (HMLR) on Point Cabrillo, Pacific Grove,
California using SCUBA. The C. canaliculatum were most often

found feeding on the blades of the brown alga Macrocystis

pyrifera and occasionally on the red alga Gigartina

corymbifera on the sandy bottom.
