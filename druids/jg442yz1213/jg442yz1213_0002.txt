Materials and Mehtods:

Distribution in Monterey Bay

I used video footage shot at points along the Monterey submarine
canyon wall to establish distributional patterns for Anthomastus ritteri
The footage was shot from a Sony Betacam DXC 3000 camera mounted on a
submersible Remote Operated Vehicle (ROV) operated by the Monterey Bay
Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI). In 1990, MBARI conducted canyon
wall dives at five locations, and these sites were surveyed for
Anthomastus ritter densities. Densities were established according to
the number of Anthomastusseen per unit time. The canyon wall locations
included the Point Joe, C4-C5, Canyon Wall Meander, North Wall, and Soquel
Canyon sites shown in Figure 1.

five-minute intervals. The depth, color, and habitat type were recorded
for each video segment surveyed. The three different habitat types
