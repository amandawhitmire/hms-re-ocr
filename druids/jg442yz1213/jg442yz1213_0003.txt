A total of eleven dives were surveyed. They included two dives at
Point Joe on April 19, 1989 and January 10, 1990; four dives at C4-C5 on
March 26, May 1, May 7, and May 14, 1990; one dive at Canyon Wall Meander
on February 28, 1990; three dives at North Wall on January 17, January 29,
and February 5, 1990; and one dive at Soquel Canyon on March 19, 1990.
Feeding Behavior

Anthomastusindividuals were collected with the MBARI ROV and kept
at 6°C in tanks at the Monterey Bay Aquarium. Feeding behavior was
studied using live Artemianauplii and dead krill. Live kelp mycids,
Acanthomysis insculpta, and brine shrimp, Artemia, were also tried, but
Anthomestus specimens were unable to capture and ingest these larger,
live animals. Nauplii were squirted gently near the Anthomastus
tentacles using a turkey baster. Care was taken not to disturb the polyps
with too forceful a squirt from the turkey baster while at the same time
ensuring the nauplii would run into the polyp tentacles. The dead krill was
placed on a tentacle using forceps. The esophagus of the Anthomastusis
clearly visible as a red tube running through the transparent outer tissue
layers along the length of the anthocodia. The movement of krill down the
esophagus can be observed because the black eyes of krill are visible
through the red tissue layer lining the esophagus.
