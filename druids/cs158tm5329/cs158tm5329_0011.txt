Benthic algae and phytoplankton are subjected to marine
pollutants over oceanic seasons and long term exposure might
increase inhibition of photosynthesis at low concentrations,
damaging internal cellular enzymes systems, decreasing
productivity capacities. Selective toxic stress might

alter species composition of the algae populations and
produce flora imbalance.
