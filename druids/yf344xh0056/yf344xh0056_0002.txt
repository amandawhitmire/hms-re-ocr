Abstract

The herbivorous marine planktonic copepod Calanus
helgolandicus was incubated in sea water which contained

c14-DDT or phytoplankton labeled with c14-DDT to study

the entrance of DDT into the marine food web at a low

trophic level. The copepods concentrated c14-DDT by a factor
of about 10,000 in 24 hours and continued to take up c14-DDT

from low levels in the water in ten-day experiments.

c14 -DDT was also found in copepods which had been fed

c14-DDT-containing phytoplankton.
