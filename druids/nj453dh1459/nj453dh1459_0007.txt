Transplants

Methods

Painted R-1 impets and P-limpets were transplanted to Pollicipes
clusters of size 50 Pollicipes or more. The limpets remaining on each
cluster were counted one day later, and an area of 1 m radius was checked
for marked limpets. Where controls were used, equal numbers of tested
and control limpets were transplanted.
