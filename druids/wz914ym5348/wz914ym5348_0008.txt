TSTX-Kα block in Sf9 cells is similar to that in GFL neurons.

Although maximum block in Sf9 cells expressing only SqKv1A
channel cDNA is less than in GFL (45% block vs. 86% in GFL), the kinetics
of TsTX-Kα block is similar, with half block occurring after 2-3 minutes (20
K+ ext., pH 7.2, 18°C) (Figure 5A). Similarly, inactivation was found to be
faster in TsTX-Kα (t = 45 ms vs. 58 ms in control) (Figure 5B), but this effect
was not as strong as in GFL neurons. Degree (i.e. completeness) of
inactivation is not much affected by TSTX-Ka. Since Sf9 cells express only
the SqKv1A channel type, the similarity to TsTX-Ka block of GFL IK
suggests that its effects are specific to a single channel type in squid GFL

neurons .
