The mean pheophytin-a/ chlorophyll-a ratios show a
negative correlation with the division rates, as shown in
Figure 4. This is to be expected, as a population with a
high amount of senescent zooxanthellae would show a low
division rate and a high amount of degenerate cells, hence
a high pheophytin concentration. Furthermore, the negative.
correlation indicates that the pheophytin-a present in the
tissues is not caused by a continual leakage from the algal
cells, which would occur in the case of a turnover of chloro-
phyll in those cells.
