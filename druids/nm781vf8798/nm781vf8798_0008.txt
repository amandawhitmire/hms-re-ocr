The pheophytin-a which is released by the degenerating
algal cells provides a good indication of other compounds
which are also released to the anemone tissue for possible.
Bonner (1950) reports that chlorophyll is stable in the
chloroplasts only when in association with other chloroplastic
materials, such as chloroplastic proteins. When the alga
degenerates, the chloroplast is the first organelle to lyse,
spilling its contents to the anemone. Thus, an accumulation
of the pheophytin-a could indicate an accumulation of other
