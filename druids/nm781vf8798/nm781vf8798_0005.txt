Zooxanthellae were found to have 2.5x105 mg of chloro-
phyll-a per algal cell. In addition, 2.15mg of pheophytin-a
was found in the same population which contained approximately
4.5x105 zooxanthellae. Cultured zooxanthellae were determined
to have 37.9x105 mg of protein per algal cell.

Observations were made during the course of this investi-

gation regarding the extrusion of zooxanthellae from the

anemone. Muscatine (1974) reports periodic extrusion of a

mucus containing zooxanthellae from the mouth. Possible

sources for these zooxanthellae are the mesenteries, as reported
by Taylor (1968), and the large mass of zooxanthellae which

I observed in the lumen of the tentacles.
