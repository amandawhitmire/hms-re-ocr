First, consider the chlorophyll-a assay for the various
tissue areas. From the reports of Bogorad (1962), these
relative amounts give a reasonable indication of the number of
zooxanthellae in the various tissues. These amounts remain

relatively stable throughout the five anemones. This stability
is probably not due to the time of day at which the anemones
were collected and assayed, because anemone #1 was kept in less
than normal light for a week and still compares well with the
mean values.

The division rates give some idea as to the movement of
the zooxanthellae within the anemone. These are apparent
division rates, and are not meant to suggest that the algal
cells are necessarily dividing any more rapidly in any portion
