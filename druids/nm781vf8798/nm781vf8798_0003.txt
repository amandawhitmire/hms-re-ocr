MATERIALS AND METHODS

All anemones were collected intertidally from Hopkins
Marine Station. In the field, they were oriented such that
each received approximately the same amount of sunlight, and
all except anemone #1 were collected at about 9:00 A.M. of the
day they were to be assayed.
Anemone #1 remained in an aquar-

ium with less than normal light for one week before being
assayed .

Assays for chlorophyll-a and pheophytin-a were conducted
on anemones #1 through #5 by homogenizing separately the
following tissues: tentacles, mesenteries, oral disc, upper
column, and middle column. Pigments were extracted with acetone.
Optical density readings were made at the appropriate wave-
lengths on a Unicam (model SP600) spectrophotometer.

Amounts

of pigment were determined by the formulas stated in Strickland
and Parsons (1968).

Division rates were determined by macerating the various
tissue areas in 1M acetic acid and counting the number of
dividing algal cells per 100 cells in a random field. No
distinction was made between senescent or degenerate cells.
and viable cells in the count. Anemones #6 through #10 were
used for these observations.
