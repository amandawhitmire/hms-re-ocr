Growth Studies of Tegula funebralis, and
Acmaea asmi

From September, 1963 to May, 1964, caged.

populations of Tegula funebralis and of its com-

mensal Acmaea asmi were measured bi-weekly in an

attempt to determine their growth rates and the seasonal
flucuations, if any, of these rates. The growth rate
of each animal was followed individually. The

animals remained, so far as possible, in their natural

environment .

The following is a brief summary of our work;
the paper is divided into three sections: (1) pre-
liminary work which included design, construction.
and installation of cages; (2) methods of marking
and measuring the animals and consideraбions given
to proceedure; and (3) data and conclusions.
