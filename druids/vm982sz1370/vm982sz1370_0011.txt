we attempted to retain the ecology of their natural
environment, it is possible that neither T. funebralis
nor A. asmi were "happy" in the dage's. Although the
animals had access to a lush algae rock covering, it
is possible that some dietary element was lacking and
that this was the limiting factor to their growth.
Lack of free movement from rock bottom to top,

lack of adequate water circulation, possible contamination
from the cages, could have further limited the growth
rates. It should be noted that the growth rates of
very small animals was not noted; one could asssume
that these would be far more rapid.
