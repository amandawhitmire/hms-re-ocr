Frog Kv1.1 and Kv1.3 channels are insensitive to a 1:1000 dilution of milked venom. As
described in methods, families of Ik traces at different voltages were recorded in the absence and
presence of a 1:1000 dilution of C. striatus milked venom in ND96. Figure 2a and 2c are plots
of the peak Ik versus voltage for Kv1.1 and 1.3, respectively. These graphs demonstrate that for
a representative cell, there is no significant effect of milked venom on either Kv1.1 or 1.3. In
order to average the results of multiple experiments (n=3 Kv1.1, n=2 Kv1.3), potassium
conductance (gk) was plotted versus membrane voltage. Conductance was calculated from the
relation Iк= gk (V-VK), where V is equal to the membrane voltage and VK is the reversal
potential for Ik. Given the technical difficulties of determining reversal potential of Ik in a
Xenopus oocyte, VK was approximated with the Nernst equation.
