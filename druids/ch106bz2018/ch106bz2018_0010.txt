Summary

Populations of Acmaoa nolta (Eschscholtz, 1833) exhibit
variation in sholl shape and color. Field studies showed

correlations of these variables with alcal

association, substrate, and exposuro. Shell shape seems to
bo donondont upon substrate and exposure. Sholl color may
bo related to diot, but preliminary investigations of rut
carbohydrases failed to reveal any distinctive quantitative
differences in the amylase, fucoidinase and alginase found
in the A. polta forms assayed.
