Results

Diet Analysis

Based on data from the bomb calorimetry analysis, the total caloric
content and percent composition of different food types was shown to vary
greatly towards a higher percentage of fat in sardines. Figure 1. The LFD was
composed mainly of squid and smelt while the HFD was composed only of
sardines. Though each diet was of equivalent caloric value (28 kcal/kg/day), the
LFD was 1% fat (<1 kcal of fat/kg/day) as compared to the HFD which was 8%

fat (2.56 kcal/kg/day). Figure 2.
