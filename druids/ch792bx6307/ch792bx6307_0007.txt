Discussion

Potts (1967) reports that amino nitrogen is one of the
major components of non-protein nitrogen lost from marine
molluscs. He suggests that free amino acids are lost mainly
through diffusion across the body wall. Spitzer (1937)
reports that Mytilus edulis (Linnaeus, 1758) loses

.1-1.0 mg/100g/day. of amino nitrogen, while Modiolus demissus
loses 1.4 mg/100g/day.

The Mopalia muscosa in Figure 1 lost .48 mg/100g/day
of what appears to be amino nitrogen, assuming 220 μg of
nitrogen to be the maximum level released over the 24 hour
period. Another M. muscosa with significant ninhydrin
positive results lost .60 mg/100g/day of amino nitrogen.
