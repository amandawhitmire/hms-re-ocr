FIGURE 9: Calcium wave propagation in bradykinin stimulated cells. Ca2+ rise begins with a
delay of 10-11 seconds after agonist stimulation (time=0) and spreads across the cell in a defined
wave-front (varying colors represent varying levels of fluorescence). Two separate waves
originate at opposite ends of the cell (soma and growth cone) and propagate toward one another
at 42μm/s. Upon collision, the waves annihilate each other (arrow).

FIGURE 10: Flash photolysis of IP3 does not cause wave propagation. Following flash
photolysis (time=0), fluorescence (black) increases evenly throughout the cell, indicating
simultaneous Ca2+ release and a lack of wave propagation.
