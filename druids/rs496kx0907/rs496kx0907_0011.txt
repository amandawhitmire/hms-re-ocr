Cleaning Reagents:

SLIK-A-WAY was the most toxic reagent tested (0.001ml with or

without 1g crude killed the entire sample overnight). Next were Corexit
9550, 9527 and Nutri-Bio; 0.001ml of the solutions killed all the samples
by the fourth day (one day sooner than 1g crude). Nokomis was slightly
less toxic followed by Corexit 9580 (when it did not disrupt oxygen
exchange) then Corexit 7664.

Corexit 7664 had the lowest toxicity to T. californicus. Past studies
have shown that it has had small negative effects on copepods (Venezia
and Fossato 1977). Verily, 0.3ml Corexit 7664 by itself was mildly toxic,
killing all the samples within 12 days. The hypothesis that there is a
positive synergistic effect between Corexit 7664 and crude oil
(Elgershuizen and De Kruijf, 1976) was false only in the experiment with
0.5ml crude oil and 0.3ml Corexit 7664, since this sample died overnight
and 0.5ml crude usually takes 4 days to kill 7. californicus Otherwise,
0.3ml Corexit 7664 prolonged the life of the 1g crude samples from 5 to
15 days and the sample with a 3 gram tar ball from 7 to 11 days.
