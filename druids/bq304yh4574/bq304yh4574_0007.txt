Voltage Dependence of Toxin Binding

The KD of toxin binding was calculated at each voltage using the

equation :

KD = toxin [Toxin]/(I control + toxin)

Where control represents the current when no toxin is present after a
500 ms pulse and toxin represents the current in the presence of toxin
after a 500ms pulse. The KD of toxin binding increases as voltage
increases, as is shown in fig. 3. The increase in K is similar in both

the wild type and H351G channels.
