Materials and Methods

SqKv1A channels containing a G87R mutation where expressed in
Xenopus oocytes by cRNA injection as previously described by

Rosenthal and Bezanilla(1997). After 2-3 days currents between 5-
30 μA could be recorded from wild type channels. Currents from
H351G mutants were a little smaller. Currents were recorded by the

two electrode whole cell voltage clamp method using p/4 leak
subtraction. Only oocytes that had a small leak (under -0.2 μA at -
60 mv holding potential) and stable K+ currents of at least 4-5 μA

were used.

Electrodes were filled with 3 M KCI, and the external bath

solution was either ND-96: 96 mM NaCl, 2 mM KCI, 1 mM MgCl2, 1.8
mM CaCl2, and 5 mM Hepes or a solution containing 90mM NaCl, 10
mM KCl, 2 mM CaCl2, 5 mM MgCl2, 10 mM Hepes at pH 7.15. For the
