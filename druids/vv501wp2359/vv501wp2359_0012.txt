Figure 1. Sensitivity of PCR for detecting pGFLN1. Each lane contains a serial
dilution of PGFLN1 which was amplified by PCR. Lanes 1 and 8 each consist of a
one kilobase DNA ladder, while lanes 2-7 and 9-13 constitute the serial dilutions of
PGFLN1. Lane 2 was loaded with the most concentrated sample of pGFLN1 and
corresponds to 1x10^-2 ug or 1.11x10^9 molecules of PGFLN1. Each subsequent lane
contains a ten-fold dilution of pGFLN1. Lane 14 is the negative control. The band
measures 921 kb.

Figure 2. Specificity of PCR for detecting GFLN1 cDNA. PCR was performed with
GFLN1 CDNA made from RNA isolated from different squid tissues/stages. Lane 1,
the positive control, contains 1x10^-8 ug of pGFLN1. The negative control was run
in lane 2 and contains no DNA template in the PCR mix. Lanes 3-5 each represent
3 ug of RNA extracted from adult stellate ganglion (SG), adult gill tissue, and stage
23 embryos (23), respectively. The molecular size of the amplified GFLN1 segment
is 921 kb.

Figure 3. Detection of GFLN1 cDNA by PCR from staged squid embryos. The
numbered lanes indicate the embryonic stage from which RNA was extracted. Each
lane represents 3 ug of RNA that was made into cDNA and amplified in PCR.
RNA from adult stellate ganglion (SG) and pGFLN1 (PL) served as positive controls
for detection of GFLN1. The molecular weight of the amplified region of GFLN1 is
921 kb.

Figure 4. RNAse Protection Assay with GFLN1-specific probe. Hybridization
occurred between 10 ug RNA per embryonic stage and 3.33x10^4 cpm of probe.
Undigested probe (P) was loaded at 1000 cpm and measured 267 bp. The protected
sodium channel sequence measured 237 bp in length.
