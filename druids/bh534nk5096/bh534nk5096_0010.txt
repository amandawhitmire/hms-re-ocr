DISCUSSION

We constructed our RNA probe from a partial sequence obtained from the cDNA
library of the stellate ganglion. Although an RNase protection assay showed that Sqẞ
was expressed in the stellate ganglion, we were unable to locate significant amounts of
Sqẞ in this tissue by in situ hybridization. Sqẞ may be present in low abundance in the
stellate ganglion.

Because we lacked the N-terminus which is the primary determinant of the type

of ẞ subunit, we compared our sequence with those in the literature in an attempt to
classify Sqẞ. Aside from the N-terminal, differences between rat Kvẞ1 and rat Kvẞ2 are
primarily scattered point differences. (Figure 9) However, many of these point
differences are conserved when comparing rat and bovine ẞ2. Our partial clone
matched the rat and bovine ẞ2 in 14 sites where rat and bovine ẞ2 were identical but
different from rat ẞ1. The opposite case, where our Sqẞ matched rat ẞ1 where rat and
bovine ẞ2 were the same, only occurred at 5 sites. Thus, it appears to be likely that
Sqẞ belongs to the ẞ2 family.
