E. hedgepethi did not fare well on a diet of

isolated C. fragile chloroplasts. In fact, it appears

that they continued to starve during this period. Chlorophyll
content was 10 times lower than in slugs assayed at the end
of the 18 day starvation period, while fixation was half.
This low fixation value is comparable to values reported
by Greene (1970a) for fixation by Plachobranchus
ianthobapsus when incubated in the dark. Apparently

E. hedgepethi is not able to recognize and/or eat
isolated chloroplasts.
