b. Chemical factors

In these experiments I attempted to discern whether an
extract made from grinding up bodies, shells, or tissue of
Chthanalus dalli accelerated hatching, as found by Crisp and
Spencer (1958) for Balanus balanoides. Extracts of C. dalli

were prepared by grinding the hole animal in a teflon
homogenizer in artificial sea water and them centrifuging
to remove any particulate matter. Two lamellae were taken
from the same animal and washed several times in artificial
sea water. They were then placed on microscope slides
and a drop of extract plus artificial sea water was placed
on one and a drop of artificial sea water on the other.
Hatching in both lamellae was observed under the compound
microscope and no consistant differences in the time of
release of the first nauplii were noted. The number of
nauplii released after 30 to 60 minutes was also not sig-
nificantly different.
