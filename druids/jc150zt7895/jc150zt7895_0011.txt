All experiments and calibrations were performed at
21°C. To compensate for minor temperature fluctuations
during an experiment (a one degree change resulted in as
much as a ten per cent change in current flow), the 02
tension at two-three different sites on the animal were

simultaneously measured with two-three different cathodes.
In each experiment the animal, previously kept in
the desired environment for a minimum of four hours, was
placed upside-down and the cathodes inserted into the
desired tissue or circulatory vessels, through holes pre-
viously made with a dissecting needle. The cathodes were
left in place and supported in small ring stands, whereas
the anode was placed on the tissue only at the time of

measurement .
