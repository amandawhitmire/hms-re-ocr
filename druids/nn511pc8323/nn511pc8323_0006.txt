Summary

1. An account is given of experiments designed to detect
proteinases in the digestive gland of the marine gastropod,
Littorina planaxis and to characterize these enzymes with respect
to pH optima, activation by dibasic cations and a reducing agent
and ammonium sulfate fractionation.

2. Activity of a crude tissue extract at various hydrogen
ion concentrations suggests the presence of at least two kathepsin-
like proteinases having pH optima at 4.9-5.1 and 5.5.

3. Neither the metallic cations Mn, Ni, Co, at 1 X 10-4 M,
nor the reducing agent NaCN at 1 X 10-3 M markedly inhibits or

activates either of the above.
