Results and Discussion

The proteinase activity with respect to pH is represented
in Figure 1. Two activity peaks, having optima at pH 4.9-5.1
and at pH 5.5, suggest the presence of at least two distinct
enzymes with slightly acidic pH optima. Proteinases active
in this range have been detected in many invertebrates and are
vaguely characterized as extracellular or intracellular kathepsins
(Nicol, 1960). Neither the metallic ions nor the sodium cyanide
markedly activated or inhibited the activity of the enzymes.
