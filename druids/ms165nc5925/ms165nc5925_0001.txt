It is apparent that the disposaloofs sewage in

level is raised, the times of maximum input, and the
persistence of the organic carbon have been unclear.
Such matters are of importance for two reasons: 1) Some
of the organic carbon introduced by the sewage is in
particulate form and invites the attachment and prolif-
eration of bacteria; 2) if sewage deposits a great excess
of organic carbon, there will be an increased demand on
oxygen for its oxidation and an accompanying stress on
aerobic organisms. The objectives of this study have
been consonant with the above considerations. Readily
oxidizable organic carbon was measured by its ability
to reduce dichromate.
