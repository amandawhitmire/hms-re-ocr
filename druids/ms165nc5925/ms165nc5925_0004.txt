The BOD increased with time in sewage diluted

1/10. This increase suggests that the amount of material
susceptible to rapid microbial oxidation increases with
time. This increase in BOD and periodic increase in
dichromate-oxidizable material indicate that depolymer-
ization or partial oxidation by microbial action makes the
remaining organic material more susceptible to microbial
oxidation and dichromate oxidation as well. This effect
is less obvious at higher dilutions of sewage.
