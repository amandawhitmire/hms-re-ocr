Forskolin consistently increased the width at half maximal height of
action potentials: from 10 m5 to 30 mS. It consistently caused a decrease
in height of action potentials: from 85 mV from peak to bottom of
hyperpolarization to 65 mV, and in one out of four trials, it noticeably
increased action potential frequency during a stimulus.

CPT CAMP was added in concentrations of 1 micromolar. At this
concentration, no effects were observed measuring action potential
frequency, height, or width. Resistance of the cell also did not change.
Four experiments were done with CPT CAMP. All yielded the same results.
Adding TPA at a concentration of 50 nM to cells in ASW produced no
noticeable effect on action potential frequency and no consisted effects on
action potential height or width. Adding TPA to cells in Na*-free sea
water stimulated action potential production over control levels at low
voltages. Cells in Na*-free sea water had resting potentials of
approximately -70 mV. At this potential, pulses of up to 5 nA produced no
action potentials in control cells. Cells treated with TPA however,
produced low frequency firing as a result of these pulses. When cells were.
