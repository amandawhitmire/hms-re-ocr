DISCUSSION

As larger barnacles receive larger wave forces than
smaller ones due to a greater exposed area, one would expect
that they would have to be attached with a greater tenacity

in order to survive.

Unexpectedly though, the results

obtained here do not support this line of reasoning. Only
barnacles attached to one of the four types of rock

demonstrated a significant relationship between force and
