Materials and Methods

Loligo Opalescens, the animals used in this study, were

obtained from Monterey Bay, Monterey, Ca. They were maintained

in large circular holding tanks (ca. 2m in diameter and im deep).
The experimental animals were typically 7 to 12 cm in mantle length.
Because of the nature of the in vivo preparation, only healthy,
strong swimming animals with all skin intact were chosen.
