Other fixing and staining techniques:

Carnoys was also used without DAPI. Eggs suspended in FSW
were centrifuged and the FSW removed by aspiration. The eggs were
resuspended in carnoys solution for one hour before being washed-
with 45% acetic acid. The eggs were viewed under a phase

microscope .

A detergent-extacted-DAPI prdocedure was also used to
visualize the chromosomes of the egg (Dr. Daniel Mazia), Eggs were
centrifuged in FSW and the pellet resuspended in KGE solution of pH
6.8 (.29M potassium gluconate, 306M glycine,2mM potassium ethylene
glycol bis-(B-aminoethylether) N,N,N',N'-tetraacetic acid,KEGTA,
2mM MgSO. Stock solution of EGTA is dissolved by titrating with KOH
to about pH 6.5). The eggs were centrifuged again and resuspended in
detergent-DAPI solution (made with ten volumes of KGE-pH 5.8 and
one volume 10% Triton X-100). Then DAPI was added to make a final
concentration of 1 ug/ml. The stained eggs were viewed under a
fluorescent microscope.
