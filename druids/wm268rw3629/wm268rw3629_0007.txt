Nuclear events:

The carnoys-DAPI technique was used to visualize the egg and
sperm chromosome structure before and after fertilization. Portions
of newly fertilized eggs were fixed with carnoys every five minutes
up to the first cleavage. Unfertilized eggs were found to be in
arrested metaphase 1 (Figure 1). They had not yet gone through the
meiotic divisions that produce polar bodies. Within five minutes
after sperm addition, cells had barely begun to enter anaphase I
(Figure 2). Between five and fifteen minutes after fertilization, the
zygote had formed one polar body (PB) which stayed intact at the edge
of the zygote (Figures 3 and 4). At twenty five minutes, the meiotic
apararus of anaphase II occured next to the site where the first PB
was formed (Figure 5). Between twenty-five and thirty-five
minutes after fertilization, the second PB had formed and remained at
the outer edge of the zygote next to the first PB (Figure 6) Sixty
minutes after fertilization, the zygote was already dividing (Figure
7). The mitotic events must have been rapid, as they were missed.
