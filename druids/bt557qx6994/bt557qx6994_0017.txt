CONCLUSIONS

Under Mg2+-free environment, TRPM7 shows high affinity to Ca2+ influx. However,
rather than an entry pathway for Ca2+, TRPM7 is more important as a transporter of
Mg2+ at physiological levels of Ca2+ and Mg 2+. Considering that TRPM7 is ubiquitous in
many different types of cells and its role in Mg2+ homeostasis, TRPM7 may be a critical
to life. Role of TRPM7 in regulation of Mg2+ implicates its importance in cellular
metabolism. The study of Mg2+ regulation through the TRPM7 is a promising avenue for
pharmaceutical drugs that target many types of diseases.
