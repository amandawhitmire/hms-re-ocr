Measurements of intracellular changes in fluorescence (as an indicator of Ca2+
influx) was carried out using the Ca2+ sensitive indicator Fluo-3-AM under a laser
confocal microscope. Cells grown on glass coverslips were transferred to small
petri dish to be loaded with Fluo-3-AM (5µL). Cells are loaded with the dye for 40
minutes at room temperature, then washed for 5 minutes on a rotator. Bathing
solution is a standard solution of the following composition (mM): NaCI 140, KCI
2.8, HEPES NaOH 10, CaCl2 2, MgCl2 2, Glucose 10, pH 7.2. CaCl2 and MgCl2
concentrations were adjusted based on the specific experimental test.
Adjustments are as indicated in the text and figure legends. In specific solutions.
of 0 mM Ca2+/0 mM Mg 2+, 0 mM Ca2+/2 mM Mg2+, 2 mM Ca2+/0 mM Mg 2+, NaCl
concentration is adjusted to 145mM to correct for osmolarity.

1mM GdCl2 stock solution was obtained from Dr. Bill Gilly's lab. This was
adjusted to 20μM using the specific Ca2+/Mg2+ solution in the experimental run.

To obtain maximum fluorescence for calibration, 1 mM ionomycin was added into
1mL of either 2 mM Ca2+/0 mM Mg2+ or 2 mM Ca2+/2 mM Mg2+ solution to obtain
2μM ionomycin solution, which was then added at the end of each experimental
run. Calibration to a maximum fluorescence allows us to look at compare
fluorescence changes between different experimental conditions and between

the induced and non-induced cells
