MATERIALS AND METHODS

Animals

Samples of the green variety of I. montereyensis
and I. kirchanskii were collected from beds of the
eelgrass, Phyllospadix scouleri, both at Tomales
Bay, Marin County, California and at Seventeen Mile
Drive, Monterey County, California. Samples of the

brown coloured variety of I. resecata were collected
from the kelp Macrocystis pyrifera off the Hopkins
Marine Station, Pacific Grove, California.
