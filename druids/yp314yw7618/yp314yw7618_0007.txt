Ribosomal DNA probes were used to determine whether probes designed
specifically for the AN gland symbiont would hybridize to the egg sheath isolates.
Bacterial cells were harvested from 1ml of log phase cultures of three morphologically
different egg sheath isolates, and two control strains. The harvested cells were washed in
cold sterile seawater and then resuspended in 1ml cold sterile seawater. This resuspension
was added to 1ml of 8% paraformaldehyde fixative (4g paraformaldehyde flakes, 5mls
10xPBS, 45mls ddH₂O) and fixed for 1 hour. After 1 hour the cells were pelleted from the
fixative, washed in sterile seawater, and resuspended in cold sterile seawater. 30ul of the
fixed cells were applied to slides coated with gelatin and chrome alum CrK(SO₁). The
slides were allowed to air dry, and then were post-fixed for 20 minutes in a solution of
30mls 37% formaldehyde and 270mls MeOH. After the post-fixation the slides were
rinsed briefly in water and dried in the dark.
