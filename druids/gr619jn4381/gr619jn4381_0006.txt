Pattern of Distribution.

Corallimorphs were commonly seen at North Wall, Soquel Canyon,

the Meander sites, C4-C5, Anthomastus Ridge. There was only one seen on
the wall at Point Joe. Corallimorphs were not seen at Point Lobos, the
Monterey Canyon Head, Cypress Point or the Kelp study sites of Point Joe
(36.190 m, 357.840 m) and Carmel Bay (106.070 m, 446.500 m) at which
level areas rather than the canyon wall were searched; they have been
sighted at these depths at other sites (Tables 1 & 2).

The greatest populations of corallimorphs were seen at C4-C5 and
North Wall. Footage from tapes 10/26/89/11 and 10/26/89/12 show a
patch of approximately 216 anemones between the depths of 351.430 and
356.010 meters at a North wall site. This dense population of anemones
was distinctly different from other sightings. Table 5 compares number
of solitary anemones to the number of anemones which appeared to be
clonal, all anemones seen at the North wall patch were listed with the
clonal anemones.
