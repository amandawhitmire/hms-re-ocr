The mammalian CNS, however, is too intricate and complex
to study the effects of glutamate in viva The purpose of this study was
to determine whether neurons of marine surfperch Cymatogaster
aggregate possess glutamate receptors, and in addition, whether these
neurons are susceptible to GNT. Marine surfperch might then serve as a
simple model in which to study the phenomenon. I report here that
glutamate receptors may be expressed on surfperch CNS neurons and that
glutamate is a potent neurotoxin in vitro.
