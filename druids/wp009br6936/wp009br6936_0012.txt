FIGURE LEGENDS

Figure 1. Glutamate receptors are expressed by neurons as early as Day 1
in culture. Percentages of cell viability of cultures exposed to 1 mM
glutamate (lower line, +) were plotted against percentages of the control
(upper line, O). Cell viabilities were already considerably lower than
control values on Day 1. The relatively high cell viability for Day 2 of
the culture exposed to glutamate could be explained by a high proportion
of cerebellar neurons, which are known not to express glutamate
receptors in mammals.

Figure 2. Glutamate neurotoxicity is cumulative. Control cultures had
viabilities of 80% on Day 2 in culture. The higher the concentrations
used, the greater the cell death observed.

Figure 3a. Neurons in cell culture were identified by their circular or
oval cell bodies, from which processes extended.

b. Neurons were found in isolated areas, connected in groups, or,

as in this figure, connected to a mat of glial cells.
