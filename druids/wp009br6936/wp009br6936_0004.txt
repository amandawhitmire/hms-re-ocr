4) The lobes were manually minced with sterile forceps into small

5) The method for best results was as follows:

Mature marine surfperch were sacrificed in an icewater bath, submerged
in a 1:10 dilution of household bleach, and sterilized topically with 75%
ethanol. The brain was then removed and preserved in a sterile solution
of Hanks salts (Sigma) in which the salt concentration was increased to
200 mM NaCl. The salt solution (pH 7.2) was buffered with 10 mM Hepes.
The brain lobes were then minced and transferred into a solution of
1.25% dispase (30 mg. dispase in 2.5 ml. Hanks) and stirred at 25 °C. for
1 hour. Subsequently the cells were plated for 30 minutes onto
autoclaved coverslips which had been coated with poly-L-lysine for 3
hours and rinsed with distilled water. After 1/2 hour, the plated cells.
were placed in a 3 ml. bath of growth medium and incubated overnight at
17 °C. The growth medium consisted of L-15 Leibovitz Medium (Gibco),
5% fetal bovine serum, 1% L-glutamine Penicillin-Streptomycin, and
200mM NaCl at pH 7.4. The medium did not initially contain any
glutamate, except in the serum, which according to the supplier's
information, contained less than 20 μM.
