Quantitative determination of neuronal injury or loss versus
glutamate concentration, measured in several cultures of a single

by exposure to 1 mM glutamate, a considerable number of apparently

excluded trypan blue and remained morphologically similar for several

Discussion

The present studies indicate that glutamate receptors exist.
on the cell membranes of marine surfperch neurons, and that glutamate
is a neurotoxin in cell culture. In the concentration-effect relationship
for GNT on CNS neurons, as little as 100 μM glutamate can cause 45%
cell death. This concentration of glutamate is only 1% of the 10 mM
concentration normally found in whole mammalian brain (Waelsch, 1951).
