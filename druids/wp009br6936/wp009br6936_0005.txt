Glutamate Exposure. Prior to glutamate exposure, the cultured cells.
were examined under phase contrast 600X and photographed after various
lengths of time in culture.

During glutamate studies, the cells were bathed in the

control Hanks solution, to which various concentrations of glutamate
were added for 5 minutes at room temperature. In a triple exchange, the
cells were then washed in sterile Hanks (effective dilution = 300),
placed in 3 ml. growth medium, and returned to incubation at 17 °C.

dye normally excluded by healthy cells, and examined under phase
