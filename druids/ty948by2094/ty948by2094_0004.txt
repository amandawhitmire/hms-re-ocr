Materials and Methods:

Materials: NADH2, pyruvate, L-arginine and octopine dehydrognase were
purchased from Sigma.

Handling of gametes: Gametes were collected by injecting Strongylocentrus
purpuratus with 1 ml of 0.5 ml KC1 intracoelomically. Eggs were collected
into 15° C sea water, filtered through a 90 micron mesh, diluted with filtered
seawater to a 2% suspension, and kept suspended by constant stirring with a
motorized paddle. Dry sperm was collected with a Pasteur pipette and
stored at 5° C

Fertilization procedure: Fertilized eggs were obtained by addition of a

1\10,000 dilution of sperm, then were allowed to stand 8 minutes and kept
suspended by aeration with a Pasteur pipette.

Permeabilization Procedure: Both fertilized and unfertilized eggs were then
washed twice in Ca2+ artificial seawater, washed once in permeabilization
medium prepared according to Swezey and Epel (1988) then concentrated to
a volume of 1 ml in the same permeabilization medium. The cells were then
permeabilized according to the method described by Swezey and Epel
(1988) Three 1000-volt pulses were used to permeabilize the eggs.
Permeabilized cells were allowed to stand for three minutes and then
centrifuged. The supernatant was collected and heated for 10 minutes at
1050. This fraction was termed the permeabilized fraction which would be
