Determination of Arginine Levels: Arginine levels in each fraction were
estimated by the method described in Gaede and Grieshaber (1975).
Readings were obtained from a Perkin-Elmer fluorescence.

spectrophotometer set at 340 nm excitation and 460 nm emittance. The
reaction was performed in a cuvette with a light path of 1 cm containing
200ul of 25mM pyruvate, 200ul of 5 M triethanolamine buffer with 25mM
EDTA, 400ul of .015 mM NADH, 5 unit of octopine dehydrogenase (in 50u1),
50ul of extract and 1.4 ml of distilled water. The final volume in the cuvette
was 2.5 ml. After the reaction reached equilibrium, 10 ul of .2 mM arginine
was added as an internal standard.
