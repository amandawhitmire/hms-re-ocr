Neither possibility #1 or #2 seems likely to be the
explanation for low rider density because they rely on foraging
and homesite selectiveness of the the C. scabra, a sturdy species

variety of locations under a wide variety of conditions. But
these possibilities are difficult to disprove because it would
entail watching the C. scabra as they mount L. gigantea shells,
an unpredictable event at best and one that would be difficult to

fecundity study of C. scabra which in the past have proved
