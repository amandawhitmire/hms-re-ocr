Field observations show that the amount of standing algal
growth on the surface of the L. gigantea shell is inversely

proportional to the number of riders present on the shell.

Fig .

5 shows this observation to be quantifiably true; the presence of
more riders implies the presence of less algae. In fact, with
two or more riders the amount of standing algae per square inch

of L. gigantea shell is unmeasurably low.

While both the L. gigantea with and without algae (i.e.
without and with riders) increase the thickness of their shell as
they increase its overall size (see Fig. 6), the presence of
less algae (i.e. more riders) did not cause any significant
change in rate of increase in shell thickness for L. gigantes.
The decreased presence of green algaes on L. gigantea with riders
does not make an apparent difference in the thickness of the L
gigantea shell.
