oxygen consumption is strongly correlated with metabolic rate
(Parry, 1977) which has been shown to be inversely related to
food availability (Parry, 1978, Branch & Newell, 1978, Davies,
1967). The lack of a decrease in respiration rates of riders on

crowded L. gigantea shells could indicate that there is
sufficient food available on L. gigantea shells, even on shells
with more than two riders and no apparent standing algae.

This food availability hypothesis is further supported by
the fact that rider C. scabra have heavier shells per body weight
than nonriders, which may indicate that they feed slower than the
thinner-shelled nonriders and thus need less algae on which to
