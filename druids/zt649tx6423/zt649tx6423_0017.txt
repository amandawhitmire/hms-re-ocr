However, the shell's available foraging area is not

that the amount of standing growth of algae decreased with an

shells with fewer riders, then the C. scabra from the more
crowded shells would have just as much algae available to them.

exposed areas, which would mean that these L. gigantea were
experiencing a greater amount of water flowing over them.
