Summary

1. The size distributions of gravid females in the
intertidal region for the two species are almost identical,
but P. granosimanus carries about twice as many egge as
P. samuelis.

2. For an animal of a given size, there is a large
variation in the number of eggs carried.

3. Gravid 1. granosimanus smaller than any in the
intertidal region are found in the subtidal regions.
4. P. granosimanus eggs increase more rap ly in
weight than do P. samuelis eggs.

5. P. bamuelis eggs increase slightly more rapidly
in size, because they become more elongated.
