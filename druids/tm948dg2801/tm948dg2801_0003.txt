MATERIALS AND METHODS

Sepia were hatched in Galveston, Texas and brought to
Hopkins Marine Station, Pacific Grove,

at around 3 months of

age. The animals used were between four and five months old at
the time of testing. Typical mantle length of the animals was 8

cm .

( 1 )

The three animals used for the study were kept in shaded,
individual aquaria with running sea water @ 15° C. Pictures of
individual animal were taken on three different backgrounds:
small grained sand substrate, (2) a uniform light gray background
and (3) a rock substrate containing rocks approximately the same
as the animal, but in a variety of tones. In an experimental
tank, the test animal was placed in a 17 cm bowl containing of
one of the three substrates and given five minutes to aclimate.
Pictures of the cuttlefish were taken with a 35mm Nikon camera
and two electric flashes held on stands on either side of the

Bize

tank .

Ektachrome 100 slide film was used. The camera was fastened

to a tripod approximately one meter above the tank. Since even
slight movement often seemed to bother the animals, the pictures
were taken in a darkened room and I stayed sitting quietly beside
the tank for the five minutes before the pictures were taken.
