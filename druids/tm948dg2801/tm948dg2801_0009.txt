Similarly, the same cuttlefish does not always respond the
same way to the same background (see figure 20). With the
exception of #3 on the gray substrate, each of the three animals
exhibited at least two different body patterns when placed on the

substrate before.

This study demonstrates a method for quantifying matching.
Overall tone of the animal can be quantified using a digital
image processor and pattern matching can be objectively

determined by the power spectra. This objective, quantative
method is especially powerful for judging matching on uniform
substrates such as sand or the light gray background.
analysis is still necessary, especially on a complex substrate
