Conclusions :

2+

In the absence of Mg2+, TRPM7 does act as a Ca2+ channel. We can further
conclude that external Mg2+ blocks Ca²+ influx and efflux through TRPM7 channels in a
dose-dependent fashion. We have estimated that the half block or possible Kd is between
100 and 500 μM of Mg2+. We can further hypothesize that it is the interaction of Mg2+ at
the binding site(s) of the channel that is slowing or blocking movement of Ca2+ through
TRPM7. Most broadly, we can propose that TRPM7 play a role in Mg2+ and Ca²+

homeostasis in the cell.
